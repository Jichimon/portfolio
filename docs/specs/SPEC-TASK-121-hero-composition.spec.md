## Intent

The hero currently prints one sentence and offers nothing to do with it. This item turns it into a three-level composition: the title as the page's only `<h1>`, a muted lead line that says what the author builds, and one anchor into the contact section. Every string stays in `resources/**`; the artboards move with the component so the design record and the page cannot disagree.

## Behaviors

### HOME-012 — the title is the page's heading · `critical` · `planned`

- **Given** the home frontmatter carries a thesis, **when** the page renders in either locale, **then** the hero prints it as the page's only `<h1>`, above the subtitle and the CTA.
- **Edge cases:** the title is the page's only h1 (the page had none before); it wraps to two lines at 1440px and does not overflow at 390px; it reads in both themes over the hero's background.
- **Governed by:** ADR-008
- **Tests:** the title/h1 e2e and the content-parity e2e.

### HOME-013 — the subtitle is the lead line · `critical` · `planned`

- **Given** the home frontmatter carries a subtitle, **when** the page renders, **then** the hero prints it below the title, in the muted lead treatment and within a measure narrower than the title's.
- **Edge cases:** an absent or blank subtitle renders no paragraph and no reserved gap; it wraps within its measure at 390px without pushing the CTA off the first screen.
- **Governed by:** ADR-008
- **Tests:** the subtitle e2e and the content-parity e2e.

### HOME-014 — the CTA is one anchor into contact · `critical` · `planned`

- **Given** the interface strings carry `home.hero_cta`, **when** the page renders in either locale, **then** the hero prints one anchor below the subtitle, labelled with that string, whose `href` is the contact section's in-page anchor; clicking it lands on `#contact`.
- **Edge cases:** the anchor is an `<a>`, not a button or a script; the `href` is derived from the nav structure; keyboard focus is visible; an absent or blank string renders no CTA; the CTA's bottom edge stays inside the first screen at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- **Governed by:** ADR-003, ADR-007, ADR-008
- **Tests:** the CTA e2e and the strings-parity e2e.

## Constraints and invariants

- **S-01:** no visible string is declared outside `resources/**`. The title and subtitle come from `home.{en,es}.md`; the CTA label comes from `ui.{en,es}.md`. An absent string means an absent block, never a placeholder.
- **The title is the page's only `<h1>`.** The page had none before this item; the existing display treatment carries the font, line-height, letter-spacing and margin, so the semantic change has no visual consequence.
- **No inline emphasis inside the title.** The hierarchy — 40/600 ink → 18/400 muted → solid CTA — is the contrast. The accent stays reserved for interaction.
- **The CTA is an anchor.** Its `href` is derived from `NAV_ITEMS`' `contact` item, not written into the hero. No JavaScript and no new behaviour module.
- **The type and spacing are the design's:** title 40/600 at max 800px, stepping to 30px and 25px; subtitle 18/400 muted at max 640px, stepping to 17px and 16px; CTA mirrors the form's submit; content top padding 300px wide and 160px narrow.
- **The artboards move in the same change.** `HomeES.dc.html` and `HomeMobile.dc.html` are regenerated by `derive.mjs`, never hand-edited.
- **No new files under `site/src/components/home/` or `site/tests/e2e/`** — both are at 6 of 6 (`S-03`).

## Out of scope

- An inline emphasis span in the title. A second content key or a Markdown split is its own decision.
- A CTA on other pages, a sticky CTA, or a second button style.
- The contact form, the Worker and the `mailto:` fallback.
- A mechanized site-vs-artboard diff. `TASK 27` is still open; this item moves the artboards and reviews the captured screenshots.
- The page's metadata `<title>`.
- Visit counting or any analytics event for the CTA; `TASK 122` owns the counter.
