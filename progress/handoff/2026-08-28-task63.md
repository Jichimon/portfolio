## What already exists, so it is not rebuilt

**The mechanism this item extends already exists in two layers — read both before assuming a gap.**

1. **`scripts/guards/lib/gate.mjs`'s `runGate`** (built by `TASK 34`/`39`, tested in `gate.test.mjs`) already derives a step's liveness from its own stdout — `countTestsRun` reads a `node:test` summary line (`"<marker> tests <N>"`) and fails any step reporting `0`, and `dependsOn` stops one broken tier from reporting as two unrelated failures. **This is the *gate-runner* half — it catches a step that silently ran nothing. It does not prove any individual step's own check-logic catches its target defect.** That second half is `T-04`'s job (every guard ships a red-path battery) and is what this item is actually about.
2. **The idiom this item generalizes already has one working instance**: `scripts/guards/lib/sources.test.mjs:57`, `"the check would catch a planted control byte"` — cited in `EC-002` as `outcome: Partial` precisely because it exists in exactly one place and was never generalized. Read it; it is the whole pattern. A test asserting "a healthy tree produces zero findings" is not enough — a **second, paired** test must assert the predicate itself catches the bad case, or a neutered check and a working one look identical.

## The scoping insight — do this triage before delegating anything

Of the 20 steps in `scripts/gate.mjs`, most are thin CLI wrappers over a `scripts/guards/gate/check-*.mjs`, each backed by its own `scripts/guards/lib/*.test.mjs`. **Under `T-04`, those already carry a red-path battery** (`check-rules-registry`, `check-terms`, `check-templates`, `check-settings`, `check-contracts`, `check-agents`, `check-procedures`, `check-trace`, `check-docs`, `check-context-budget`, `check-content`, `check-site`, `check-evals`) — verify this is actually true per step (`P-04`; do not take the count on faith), but if it is, those steps may already satisfy this item's Done clause and the work is confirming it, not building it.

**The steps that plausibly do NOT have this yet are the ones wrapping an external tool rather than a `check-*.mjs` guard** — these are where the eight named escaped defects actually lived:

No permanent proof this can't silently stop firing |
