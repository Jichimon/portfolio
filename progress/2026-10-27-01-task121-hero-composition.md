## Decisions

- **The title is the page's only `<h1>`.** The home page had none; the existing display treatment carried the font, margin and measure, so the semantic change has no visual cost.
- **No inline emphasis in the title.** The hierarchy (40/600 ink → 18/400 muted → solid CTA) is the contrast; the accent stays reserved for interaction.
- **`text-wrap: balance` was added after looking at the first capture.** The first pass broke the English title after “the” and left “around.” alone at 390px; the balanced version breaks at the comma in English and after “adaptarse” in Spanish. The behavior and line counts are unchanged; this is a rag refinement, recorded in the spec's drift log.
- **The CTA keeps `--color-bg` on hover.** The global `a:hover` would otherwise paint the label in the accent colour, which is the background the hover sets. The rule is explicit in `Hero.astro` and the artboard.
- **The CTA's href is derived from `NAV_ITEMS`.** `HomeSections.astro` asks the nav's `contact` item and the e2e derives the same value; the hero never spells `#contact`.

## Findings from validating against real state (P-04)

- **The home page had no `<h1>` at all.** The hero printed the thesis as a `<p>`; the other pages already had one. The new title fills the gap.
- **The artboards' narrow thesis rules targeted `.hero-title`**, a class that exists nowhere; the site's 40 → 30 → 25 step was not in the design record. Corrected to `.thesis` in the same change, because the CTA's narrow review sits under that line.
- **`site/src/components/home/` and `site/tests/e2e/` are both at 6 of 6** (`S-03`). The item edits `Hero.astro` and `home.smoke.spec.ts`; it adds no files there.
- **The first capture exposed the line-break problem.** The automated checks were green; the screenshot is what showed the orphaned “the” and “around.”, which is the same lesson the home item recorded: a human look finds what the checks do not.
- **The spec's content handoff was applied before the implementation.** The e2e content-parity and strings-parity tests passed in the red run, proving the keys landed; the three absence tests failed, proving the component did not yet render them.
