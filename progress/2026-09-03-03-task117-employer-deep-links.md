NFD-normalizes and strips the U+0300–U+036F combining-diacritics block (so `Avícola Sofía` → `avicola-sofia`, not a percent-encoded fragment), lowercases, collapses every run of non-alphanumeric characters to one hyphen, and trims leading/trailing hyphens (so `Banco Solidario S.A.` → `banco-solidario-s-a`). ## Decisions

- **Delegated in two sequential slices rather than one, on evidence rather than caution.** `TASK 116` was a 7-object slice and its implementer was cut by the 45-turn budget three times. `P-09`'s instruction when a slice will not fit is to cut the scope, not to hope, so this item ships as slice A (`EMP-008`, the pure derivation and its red-path tests) and slice B (`EMP-009`, the wiring and its e2e assertions). Slice A is shippable and testable on its own; slice B depends on it.
- **The anchor is derived from `company`, not authored in `resources/**`.** An explicit `anchor:` key was considered and declined: it is a second value to author per locale for something the company name already determines, and it puts a URL fragment into a content file the author edits for prose reasons.
- **Legal-suffix stripping is declined, and the ugly id wins.** `Banco Solidario S.A.` yields `banco-solidario-s-a`. Getting `banco-solidario` instead requires a list of suffixes to strip — `S.A.`, `Inc.`, `Ltd.` — which is a roster, and a roster passes forever until the first suffix nobody listed (`P-13`).
- **A collision fails the build, naming both roles**, rather than auto-suffixing. Two stints at one employer is a real possibility for this record, and a reader clicking the second card and silently landing on the first is a broken promise no test would catch. Whether two entries are two roles or one is the author's call.

## Slice B — the wiring (`EMP-009`)

- **`EmploymentEntry.astro`** declares `anchor?: string`, reads its presence with `Object.hasOwn` like every other optional prop in the file, and emits `id={anchor}` on `.employment-entry` — the role's own container, so the fragment targets what a reader recognises as "the NICE role" rather than a heading nested inside it.
- **`EmployerCard.astro`** folds the fragment in where the link is built — `cardHref` is the template-literal join of `href`, `#` and `anchor` when an anchor exists, and plain `href` when it does not. A role whose company name yields no derivable anchor still links to the plain experience page rather than to `#`, which is the degenerate case `EMP-008` deliberately produces an absent key for.
- **`EmployersSection.astro`** spreads `anchor` conditionally, the same value-blind way it already spreads `logo` and `logoDark`, so an absent anchor leaves no key on the child's props at all.
- **Two interface lines** — `content-queries.ts:649` and `EmploymentRecord.astro:15` — because the record flows through untouched at runtime but a field no interface declares does not type-check into a component.
- **Three `EMP-009` assertions per locale** in `home.smoke.spec.ts`: each card links to its **own** anchor, the target element exists on the destination page, and following a **non-first** card actually scrolls past the page top. The third is the one that tests the promise rather than the markup; the first two can drift apart while each passes alone.
